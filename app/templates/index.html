1. Class balance

Parasitized vs Uninfected counts show whether the classifier sees a fair prior. A near 50/50 split means accuracy is a trustworthy headline metric.

2. Brightness

Mean brightness tells the staining/exposure story. Parasitized cells often look darker where the parasite ring appears — brightness shifts can move the decision boundary.

3. Red / green channels

Giemsa stain pushes eosinophilic (red) and basophilic (blue-green) tones. Channel means explain why a CNN can separate infection patterns from plain RBCs.
